Lesson 2 • Sight Reading

🎼 Lines & Spaces

Notes sit either on a line or in a space between lines. Use these memory tricks:

𝄞 Treble Clef (High Notes)

• 5 Lines (bottom to top): E - G - B - D - F ("Every Good Boy Does Fine")

• 4 Spaces (bottom to top): F - A - C - E (Spells "FACE")

𝄢 Bass Clef (Low Notes)

• 5 Lines (bottom to top): G - B - D - F - A ("Good Bikes Don't Fall Apart")

• 4 Spaces (bottom to top): A - C - E - G ("All Cows Eat Grass")

Lesson 3 • Symbols

♯ ♭ ♮ Accidentals

Accidentals change the pitch of a note by half-steps (semitones):

  • ♯ Sharp: Raises the note by 1 semitone (higher).
  • ♭ Flat: Lowers the note by 1 semitone (lower).
  • ♮ Natural: Cancels a previous sharp or flat.
  • 𝄪 Double Sharp: Raises the note by 2 semitones.
  • 𝄫 Double Flat: Lowers the note by 2 semitones.

Lesson 6 • Rhythm & Time

🥁 Rhythm Pulse (Beat Values)

In 4/4 time, every note and rest symbol gets a specific number of beats:

𝅝 Whole Note = 4 Beats
Open oval with no stem. Holds a full measure!
𝅗𝅥 Half Note = 2 Beats
Open oval with a stem. Lasts half a measure.
♩ Quarter Note = 1 Beat
Filled notehead with stem. The steady heartbeat!
♫ Two Eighths = 1 Beat
Two beamed eighth notes share 1 beat (½ + ½).

Lesson 6 • Rhythm

🥁 Note Values & Beats

In common 4/4 time, note values tell you how long to hold a sound.

  • 𝅝 Whole note — 4 beats
  • 𝅗𝅥 Half note — 2 beats
  • ♩ Quarter note — 1 beat
  • ♪ Eighth note — ½ beat
Lesson 7 • Tonality

🗝️ Key Signatures

A key signature places sharps or flats after the clef. Those notes stay altered throughout the piece unless cancelled.

  • 0: C major / A minor
  • 1 sharp (F♯): G major / E minor
  • 1 flat (B♭): F major / D minor
Lesson 8 • Staff Reading

📏 Ledger Lines

Ledger lines are short extra lines used for notes higher or lower than the five-line staff.

Treble clef examples

• Middle C (C4): one ledger line below the staff.

• A5: above the staff; higher notes may need more ledger lines.
